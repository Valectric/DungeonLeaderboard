# Credits

Everything in this repository is either written for it or **CC0**, because the repository is public
and MIT licensed. A licence that forbids redistribution, or one that is copyleft, cannot travel with
it — that constraint has already cost this project one art pack and two music tracks that were
otherwise a good fit.

## Music

Both tracks are **CC0 (public domain)** from [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org). Neither author
requires credit; both are credited anyway, because it costs nothing and they earned it.

| where | track | author | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| The sixty-second raid | *Duel of the Dungeons* | **Umplix** | https://opengameart.org/content/duel-of-the-dungeons |
| Standings, shop, review, endings | *Within The Abandoned Castle* | **Spring Spring** | https://opengameart.org/content/within-the-abandoned-castle |

Both were re-encoded to Vorbis for the web build, and the calm track is a ninety-second excerpt with
short fades rather than its full eight and a half minutes — 33 MB of source became 2.1 MB shipped,
which matters on a jam page where a slow load is a lost vote. CC0 permits the edit outright.

## Sound effects

Every other sound in the game is generated at runtime by `SfxSynth` — ten procedural effects, no
audio files. That is why the game shipped without any audio licensing question until music arrived.

## Art

The pixel art is generated with a local tool and committed as source. The dungeon tiles, doors,
adventurers, monsters and the two full-screen scenes are all made for this project.
